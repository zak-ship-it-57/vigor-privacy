Vigor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026 · Atlas Little Apps

Vigor is a run, walk and bike tracker made by Atlas Little Apps ("we"). This page explains what the app does with your information. The short version: your activities stay on your phone.

What Vigor stores, and where

  • Activities and routes. When you record, Vigor uses your phone's location to measure distance, pace and your route. Location is used only while you record, and the route, stats, notes, tags, gear and settings are stored on your phone only.
  • Phone settings checks. Before you record, Vigor looks at whether location, precise location, notifications and battery saving are set so recording works, and after a recording it notes whether GPS was paused. This is read and kept on your phone only.
  • No account, no analytics, no ads. Vigor has no sign-in, does not collect analytics and shows no ads.
  • We do not receive your location or activities. We run no server that stores them for you.

Optional features that send data off your phone

  • Health Connect (optional). If you connect it, Vigor saves your finished activities (exercise session, route, distance, elevation and any strap heart rate) to Health Connect, and reads your steps and heart rate to show them in the app. If you choose "Import workouts", it also reads the workouts, distance, elevation and heart rate other apps saved there and copies them into Vigor. Health Connect is Android's on-device store; you control what Vigor may read or write and can change it any time in Health Connect. We do not send this data anywhere.
  • Sync between your phones (optional, Vigor Pro). If you turn it on, each activity is encrypted on your phone with a key that exists only on your own devices (inside the sync code you copy between them), then stored on a relay operated by Atlas Little Apps on Cloudflare. The relay holds scrambled data it cannot read. It can see the size and time of what is stored and the network address a connection comes from. "Stop syncing" in the app disconnects the phone; the encrypted copy then remains on the relay and cannot be opened without your key.
  • Bluetooth heart-rate strap (optional). If you choose a chest strap, Vigor connects to it while you record and saves its readings with the activity on your phone. Bluetooth is not used to find your location, and the readings are not sent anywhere.
  • Map tiles and offline maps. Maps are loaded from OpenFreeMap, using OpenStreetMap data. The map server sees which area of the map you are viewing, your network address and the app's name and version, as any website would. If you download an offline area, the tiles for that area are fetched once and stored on your phone.
  • Purchases. If you buy Vigor Pro, Google Play handles the payment. Vigor only asks Google Play whether this account owns Pro. We do not receive your payment details.

Your choices

  • Everything on your phone can be deleted by uninstalling Vigor or deleting activities in the app.
  • Backups and exports (GPX, TCX, CSV, backup file) go only where you send them.
  • You can stop syncing, disconnect Health Connect, or deny location access at any time. Without location access the app cannot record a route.

Children

Vigor is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Health note

Vigor is general fitness software, not a medical device, and training plans are not medical advice.
